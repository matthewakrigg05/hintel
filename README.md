# hIntel

A housing intelligence platform designed to help people make better decisions when buying or moving home in the UK.

## Overview

hIntel brings together housing market data, local area information, and analytical insights into a single platform. The goal is to reduce the time and uncertainty involved in property research by combining trusted public datasets with clear visualisations and explainable analysis.

Rather than forcing users to visit multiple websites to understand a property or area, hIntel aims to provide an easier way to answer questions such as:

- Is a property priced fairly?
- How has the local market changed over time?
- Are there signs of future price growth or risk?
- What are the costs and characteristics of the surrounding area?
- How does one area compare with another?

## Problem it solves

Buying a home requires a large amount of research across several disconnected sources. Buyers often need to check:

- property listings
- historical sales data
- local price trends
- EPC and energy efficiency information
- transport links
- demographic and census data
- school, crime, and environmental information
- mortgage and affordability considerations

This information exists, but it is fragmented and difficult to interpret. hIntel is intended to aggregate this information and present it in a way that helps users understand value, risk, and opportunity.

## Product vision

The long-term vision is to become a comprehensive housing intelligence platform for the UK market. It will combine:

- public and open housing datasets
- historical market analysis
- location-based comparisons
- machine learning models
- AI-assisted explanations
- clean, actionable reporting for home buyers and investors

The platform is designed to support confident property decisions with data, not guesswork.

## Target users

### Primary users

- First-time buyers
- Home movers

These users typically need a clear, trustworthy summary of a property or area before making a major financial decision.

### Secondary users

- Property investors
- Mortgage advisers
- Estate agents

These groups may use the platform for market comparison, trend analysis, and broader area insights.

## Core goals

hIntel aims to:

- centralise housing and local-area data
- make complex housing information easier to understand
- provide comparable market insights across locations
- highlight whether a property or area appears good value
- support explainable insights rather than black-box outputs
- create a foundation for future AI-powered guidance and recommendations

## MVP scope

The first version of the project will focus on a simple but valuable experience for users exploring an area or postcode.

Planned MVP features include:

- search by postcode, town, or local authority
- historical house price data
- average property prices by property type
- transaction history where available
- EPC statistics for an area
- transport information
- demographic and census data
- interactive charts and trend visualisations
- map-based area exploration
- REST API for retrieving housing data
- automated data ingestion and updates
- responsive web interface

## Architecture direction

The project is planned around a modern data and AI product stack:

- data ingestion pipelines for public housing and area datasets
- validation and transformation services
- relational data storage for structured market data
- analytics-ready models for querying trends and summaries
- REST API layer for application integration
- interactive frontend for search, charts, and map views
- machine learning and AI layers for forecasting and explanation
- cloud deployment with monitoring and CI/CD workflows

## Data and insight philosophy

The project prioritises:

- accurate, trustworthy data
- explainable insights over opaque predictions
- user-centric product design
- incremental delivery
- maintainable architecture
- scalability for future growth

## Project status

This repository is currently in the planning and design phase. The project direction is intentionally focused on building a real-world, portfolio-quality platform that demonstrates modern data engineering, analytics, and AI product development in the UK housing space.

## Why this matters

Home-buying decisions are high-stakes and high-friction. Many people struggle to interpret large amounts of fragmented information. hIntel is intended to make that process clearer, faster, and more evidence-based.

The project is not trying to replace estate agents or listing websites. Instead, it aims to provide a more intelligent, more analytical, and more user-friendly layer on top of the housing market data that already exists.
