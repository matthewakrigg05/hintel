# Data Requirements

The platform relies on structured, trusted data from multiple sources.

#### Housing Market Data - Land Registry

- historical property prices, direct from LR
- sales transactions, direct from LR
- price movements over time, inferred & calculated
- average price by property type and area, transformed from LR
- local market trend indicators, inferred from LR

#### Area and Geographic Data

- postcode data
- local authority and ward boundaries
- transport connectivity
- neighbourhood boundaries
- service accessibility

#### Demographic Data

- population trends
- household composition
- income or deprivation indicators
- age distribution
- housing stock profile

#### Property and Energy Data

- EPC ratings
- energy efficiency indicators
- property type classifications
- condition or building characteristics where available

#### Additional Contextual Data

- crime statistics
- flood risk, getthedata?
- school information
- green space or environmental quality
- planned developments or zoning information: https://www.api.gov.uk/mhclg/planning-and-housing-data-in-england/
