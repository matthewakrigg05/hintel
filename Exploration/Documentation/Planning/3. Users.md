# User Research

> **Note:** This document is based on publicly available information, observations of the UK housing market, online discussions and assumptions made during product discovery. It will be updated as more evidence and user feedback become available.

# Target Users

## Primary Users

### First-Time Buyers (FTBs)

**Goal**

Purchase their first home with confidence.

**Typical Questions**

* Can I afford this property?
* Is this house fairly priced?
* Is this a good area to live?
* What hidden costs should I expect?
* How much could this property be worth in the future?
* How does this compare to similar houses?

**Pain Points**

* Information is spread across many websites.
* Difficult to judge whether an asking price is reasonable.
* Limited understanding of local housing markets.
* Large financial commitment creates uncertainty.
* Many buyers have limited experience purchasing property.

---

### Home Movers

**Goal**

Find a property that better suits their changing circumstances.

**Typical Questions**

* Is moving financially worthwhile?
* Which areas offer better value?
* What compromises exist between two locations?
* How has the market changed since I bought my current home?

**Pain Points**

* Comparing different locations is time-consuming.
* Research is repetitive across multiple websites.
* Difficult to understand long-term value.

# User Problems

Current property research often requires users to switch between multiple services to answer simple questions.

Examples include:

* Property listing websites
* Government datasets
* Crime maps
* School information
* Transport information
* Mortgage calculators
* Flood risk services
* EPC information

The platform aims to reduce this fragmentation by presenting relevant information in one place.

---

# User Needs

Users need to be able to:

* Search an area quickly.
* Understand local housing trends.
* Compare multiple areas objectively.
* Assess whether a property appears fairly priced.
* Understand why insights have been generated.
* Trust the underlying data.

---

# Assumptions

The following assumptions will guide early development and should be validated over time:

* Users would value having multiple trusted datasets combined into one platform.
* Buyers prefer clear summaries over manually analysing raw data.
* Visualisations improve understanding of housing trends.
* AI-generated explanations can make complex information easier to understand, provided they are transparent and supported by evidence.
* Most users are making infrequent but high-value decisions and therefore prioritise quality over speed.

---
